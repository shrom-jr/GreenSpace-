About Our Company

Green Space
International Pvt. Ltd.

Based in Pepsicola, Kathmandu, Green Space International Pvt. Ltd. is a premier interior design and custom furniture manufacturing company. We specialize in transforming raw residential apartments, private villas, and commercial offices into luxurious, highly functional living spaces.

Under the visionary leadership of our founder, Pushkar Baral, we prioritize structural durability, modern spatial ergonomics, and client-centric pricing across every project in Nepal.
